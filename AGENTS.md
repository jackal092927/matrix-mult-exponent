# Research ownership and evidence

The public goal is an unconditional arithmetic bound omega(C) < 9/4. Read
README.md, CONTRIBUTING.md, docs/SCOPE_AUDIT.md and docs/RESEARCH_DIRECTIONS.md
before proposing a search. Preserve the pinned baseline and settled exclusions.

Each research session owns only its assigned `docs/lanes/<lane>/` and
`runtime/lanes/<lane>/` directory. The lanes are `spectrum`, `sweet-powers`,
`quotient-smoothing`, and `independent-review`. Do not overwrite another lane,
shared source documents, the verifier, or root metadata. Shared changes require
a reviewable patch and a coordinator integration. The launching chat owns the
initial public integration; no worker may accept its own mathematical result.

Canonical continuation is `runtime/lanes/<lane>/STATE.md`. Freeze a first card
before computing. Record dependencies, hypotheses, complete outcomes, costs and
negative results. Commit-worthy reports go in the lane's public docs directory;
private operational logs, session transcripts and local absolute paths stay in
ignored runtime. Public reports must remain understandable without those logs.

The earlier observatory session owns its existing Q_1 tangent audit and its
source tree. Treat it as read-only inherited evidence. This program's smoothing
lane works on structural bridges to improving k>=5, not a duplicate Q_1 run.

Start with bounded local exact arithmetic and mathematical reasoning. A remote
experiment needs a live authorized allocation and run receipt. Do not infer
authority from an idle server. Do not invoke paid model APIs. Native subscription
sessions are authorized. Do not send messages to outside people or other chats
without direct human authorization. Keep unknowns explicit and preserve source
attribution and licenses.
