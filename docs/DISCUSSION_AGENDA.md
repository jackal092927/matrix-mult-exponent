# From discussion to falsifiable research cards

This is a concise research agenda informed by a supplied discussion and checked
against primary mathematical sources. It is not a transcript or an endorsement
of every claim in the discussion. The original motivating discussion is available
at <https://chatgpt.com/share/6acd8232-cb64-83e8-ab37-a6837059a1ac>.

Three ideas are prioritized for this omega project:

1. Strengthen the polynomial-multiplication tensor profile through genuinely new
   coupled relations. The useful endpoint is a common improved bound for every
   relevant spectral character. A finite profile or one character is insufficient.
2. Turn structured low-rank search into exact tensor-power constructions with a
   proved exponent transfer. The first improving sweet-piece budget is specified
   in Lane B; small tensor rank records are intermediate facts.
3. Use structured algebra deformations to obtain a rigorously small border rank.
   Lane C isolates the sweet-piece quotient, its unknown smoothing premise and
   an improving k=5 target. A parent algebra's properties do not automatically
   descend to a quotient.

The discussion also suggests structured permutation costs on multi-tape machines,
fixed-field polynomial multiplication with extension/packing costs, and batching
small integer products without unwanted cross terms. These remain related
complexity directions, with distinct models and objectives. They are not silently
counted as progress on omega(C). Transposition and other inherited arithmetic
corollaries likewise require their own precise transfer hypotheses.

See RESEARCH_DIRECTIONS.md for executable first cards and SCOPE_AUDIT.md for the
quantifiers, thresholds and known exclusions. Every lane starts as a hypothesis.
